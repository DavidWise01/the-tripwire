THE TRIPWIRE

A monitor that halts when a metric crosses the high bound — and, crucially, stays halted until it falls back below a lower bound. The gap between them is hysteresis: it means a signal hovering at the edge can never flap the halt on and off. A stopping rule you can trust precisely because it is reluctant to un‑stop.

● ARMED — monitoring

Two thresholds: HIGH (trip) at 0.80 and LOW (rearm) at 0.55. The state is a tiny machine — ARMED until the metric exceeds HIGH, then TRIPPED until it drops below LOW, and only then ARMED again. Because the rearm bound sits below the trip bound, a value oscillating around 0.80 stays TRIPPED (safe) instead of chattering. ◆ HONEST: a faithful hysteresis / Schmitt‑trigger stopping rule on a synthetic metric stream; a demonstration of the mechanism, not a monitor wired to a real system. A self‑test verifies it trips above HIGH, holds through edge‑hover without flapping, and rearms only below LOW.
